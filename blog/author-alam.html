Creator and maintainer of MyPDF

I’m Alam, and I build MyPDF, a free, open-source PDF toolkit with 100+ tools that run entirely in your browser.

There’s no uploading your files to a server, no account to create, and no tracking. Your documents stay on your device. The source code is public, so you can see how it works yourself. MyPDF is AGPL-licensed and self-hostable.

I write every post on this blog myself. I’ll write about PDFs, how they work, things I learn while building MyPDF, and occasionally things that go wrong.

When I compare MyPDF with another PDF tool, I’ll link to its documentation so you can check what I’m saying. If there’s something MyPDF doesn’t do well, I’ll say that too.

And if I get something wrong, please open an issue. I’ll fix it and add a note to the post.

You can find me on GitHub, LinkedIn and X.
